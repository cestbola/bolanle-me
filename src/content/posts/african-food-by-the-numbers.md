A lot of what the world eats begins in Africa: the chocolate in your cupboard, the coffee in your cup and staples that feed hundreds of millions of people. Here's a tour in numbers. Some of them are quizzes, so guess before you read on.

## How much of the world's supply is African?

  - { label: Cassava, value: 63, note: "Nearly two-thirds of the world's cassava (FAOSTAT, 2023)" }

Yams are the standout. Africa grows almost all of them, and one country grows most of those. How much do you think?

```chart
type: meter
title: Quick quiz
question: What share of the world's yams does Nigeria grow?
quiz: true
labels: ["0%", "100%"]
mine: 69
why: "About 69%: 61 million of the world's 88 million tonnes in 2022, according to FAOSTAT. That's more than twice every other country combined."
```

## Where chocolate begins

Cocoa is grown across the tropics, but the harvest is concentrated in a small stretch of West Africa. Côte d'Ivoire alone grows more than the Americas, Asia and Oceania put together.

```chart
type: donut
title: Where the world's cocoa beans came from, 2022/23
unit: kt
center: "Nearly 3 in 4"
data:
  Côte d'Ivoire: 2180
  Ghana: 680
  Cameroon: 290
  Nigeria: 280
  Rest of Africa: 204
  The Americas: 1052
  Asia and Oceania: 268
caption: "Thousand tonnes. Source: ICCO Quarterly Bulletin of Cocoa Statistics, Vol. XLIX No. 4 (November 2023). Africa's share was 73.4%; later revisions put it at about 74%."
```

## The grain behind injera

Teff is a tiny grain, each seed about the size of a poppy seed, and it's the main ingredient in injera, the spongy, fermented flatbread at the centre of Ethiopian and Eritrean meals. In Ethiopia it's grown on more land than any other grain.

```chart

```

## Coffee's home country

Arabica coffee originated in the forests of south-western Ethiopia, and Ethiopia is still Africa's largest coffee producer. Many big coffee producers export most of their crop. Ethiopia keeps a remarkable share. Guess how much stays at home.

```chart
type: meter
title: Quick quiz
question: How much of Ethiopia's coffee do Ethiopians drink themselves?
quiz: true
labels: ["None of it", "All of it"]
mine: 41
why: "About 40%. USDA expects Ethiopians to drink around 5 million of the 12.1 million bags grown in 2026/27. For years it was closer to half. That's a rare thing among big coffee producers, and coffee ceremonies are a daily ritual in many Ethiopian homes."
```
