I decided to rate my energy every evening for a week. Science! Here's how it went — each emoji is how I felt that day.

```chart
type: line
title: Energy vs. motivation, one totally normal week
unit: "%"
x: [Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun]
series:
  Energy: [40, 55, 62, 48, 86, 94, 33]
  Motivation: [72, 61, 44, 36, 52, 20, 64]
markers: [😩, 🙂, 💪, 😐, 🎉, 🥳, 😴]
annotations:
  - { at: Fri, text: "payday ✨" }
caption: Motivation peaks on Monday, when I have the least energy to use it. Classic.
```

Notice how the two lines are basically *avoiding each other*. When I have energy, I don't want to do anything. When I want to do everything, I'm tired. Rude.
